Simple and Healthy Overnight Oats

Start the day off right with this easy and healthy overnight oats recipe.

Ingredients

  • 40 g oats
  • 30 g your favorite protein powder
  • 4 g flaxseed meal
  • 4 g chia seeds
  • 160 g almond milk
  • 80 g plain non-fat Greek yogurt
  • optional fresh fruit for toppings

Instructions

  1. Combine the oats, protein powder, flexseed meal, and chia seeds in your choice of container.
  2. Add in the almond milk and then mix.
  3. Top the oat mixture with the Greek yogurt and any other toppings of your choosing.
  4. Cover and store for at least four hours.
  5. Enjoy!
